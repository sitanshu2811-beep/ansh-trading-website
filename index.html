Trust and safety

A clear process you can verify

What you need to apply

  • A valid passport, at least 6 months' validity
  • Aadhaar card and passport-size photos
  • Education certificates (10th, 12th, ITI, degree)
  • Experience letters, if you have them
  • Driving licence for driver roles

Signs of a fake agent

  • No RA ID, or it isn't listed on eMigrate
  • Sends you to work on a visit or tourist visa
  • No written offer letter or contract
  • Won't let you see the employer's details
